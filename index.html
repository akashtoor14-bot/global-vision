Questions people ask first.

How is it free?

Global Vision is supported by our broker partners. If you choose to trade through a partner broker, they share part of their fee with us at no extra cost to you. That's how the signals, sessions, tools and academy stay free for everyone.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals and sessions are education. Trading carries risk and you can lose money, so only trade what you can afford to lose and make your own decisions.

Do I need experience?

No. The academy starts from the basics, and the community is there to help as you learn.

Which markets do you cover?

Mainly gold (XAUUSD), US30 and the major forex pairs, plus crypto such as Bitcoin.

What happens after I join?

You verify your email with a code, then get access to the signal desk, the live sessions and the academy. Your journal is part of the same membership.
